Working
Together.

What kind of projects do you take on?
Cross-channel copy for design-led brands — email, web, brand voice work, social, packaging. I love long-running partnerships where I can really learn a brand, but one-off campaigns are great too.
How do you work with design teams?
Closely. Copy and design need to speak the same language, so I'm in the file early and often — figma, slack, async docs, whatever the team uses. I write to the layout, not just at it.
What's your turnaround?
Depends on scope, but most single-asset pieces (an email, a landing hero) move in a few business days. Larger voice or guideline work runs on a project timeline we set together.
Do you take on freelance?
Selectively, yes. The best fit is design-driven brands with a clear voice they want to sharpen. Send a note with what you're working on and I'll get back to you within a couple of days.
